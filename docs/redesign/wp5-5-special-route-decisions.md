# WP5.5 special-route decisions

> **Historical snapshot — superseded by WP6.2.** Field Log references below describe prior state and are not current information architecture or routes.

## About

The About page is now a linear editorial narrative. The tilted manifesto, identity dashboard, faux-handwritten notes, sticky-note composition, and “currently on the desk” panel were removed. No portrait was introduced.

## Explore and search

Explore remains a discovery utility rather than a second homepage. Search keeps its existing client-side index and query behavior; only the input, result rows, metadata, and empty state were restyled to match the editorial system.

## 404

The not-found route is a concise recovery page with direct links to Home, Projects, and Search. It uses the shared shell and reading width.

## Field Log and Memories

Memories is the current public-memory surface. Field Log remains published at its existing URLs as a historical writing archive and is labeled accordingly. The two areas are linked without implying shared ownership or restoring Field Log to primary navigation.

## Project roots

- Bridget is described as a continuity, reconciliation, and orchestration system. Public Memories remains a separate publishing responsibility.
- Career Intelligence retains its narrative and supporting research/system routes inside the simplified workspace shell.
- QA Agents retains case studies, demo, live surface, system detail, Meticulous evidence, and replay behavior.
- This Website is framed as a durable design and publishing system rather than a work-package changelog.
- The Human Model and Wonderful Digital World flagships remain on their previously approved editorial compositions.

## Compost Heap

Compost Heap keeps its playful artifact archive, filters, and disclosure behavior. The page now enters through the shared breadcrumb/shell and uses current type, spacing, colors, and focus behavior. The visual exception is deliberate and contained to the artifact collection.
